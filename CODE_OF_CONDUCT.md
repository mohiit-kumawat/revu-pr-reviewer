# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

revu is a project for people with different backgrounds, experiences, and
perspectives. We want participation to be respectful, constructive, and safe
for everyone who uses, contributes to, or maintains the project.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
  project spaces.
- Give and receive feedback about ideas, code, and behavior in good faith.
- Explain disagreements clearly without personal attacks or dismissive
  language.
- Respect different viewpoints, identities, cultures, and levels of
  experience.
- Protect private information, credentials, repository content, and other
  sensitive data.
- Follow the project's contribution and security guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or targeted hostility.
- Personal attacks, trolling, insults, or sustained disruptive arguments.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Credential theft, unauthorized access, malicious testing, or attempts to
  access another person's accounts or repositories.
- Deliberately submitting harmful code, malicious payloads, or deceptive
  reports.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional project
  community.

## Reporting concerns

Do not open a public issue for urgent safety concerns, private information,
or security vulnerabilities. Contact the project maintainers through a private
channel associated with the repository. Security vulnerabilities should follow
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

When reporting a conduct concern, include what happened, where it happened,
who was involved if known, and any relevant links or evidence. Please avoid
sharing sensitive personal information or private repository content unless it
is necessary to investigate.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The project may
remove or limit access to content or participants when needed to protect the
community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take action they consider appropriate, including a
private warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent
exclusion from project spaces. Actions will be based on the available
information and the need to keep the community safe and constructive.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not
acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull
requests, reviews, project-hosted communication, and other spaces when an
individual is representing the project or materially affecting the project
community.
